The mint spec, if you want to verify it yourself

deploy → {"p":"zrc-20","op":"deploy","tick":"ghst","max":"999","lim":"1"}
mint   → {"p":"zrc-20","op":"mint","tick":"ghst","amt":"1","pow":"<nonce>"}

Proof-of-work rule: sha256(receive_address + ":" + nonce) must start with 8 leading hex zeros (00000000 right now). Not fixed — difficulty starts at 8 and adds one more zero every 300 mints, capped at 10, so it gets harder as the mint sells out. Currently 0/999 minted. Deliberately brutal — minutes on strong multi-core hardware, hours-to-days on weak ones, worse near sell-out. Anyone can re-hash the nonce in the memo and check it themselves — no trust required for this part, unlike the delivery step above.

Two ways to mint: solve the proof-of-work above and pay 0.005 ZEC (this tier's mining price — it climbs the same way the difficulty does, up to 0.02 ZEC at the top tier), or skip mining entirely and pay a flat 0.02 ZEC instead. Either way the mint stays gated by captcha + rate-limit + one-claim-per-address server-side — the proof-of-work is an extra, optional layer on top of that, not a replacement for it.
